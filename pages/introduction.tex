\Fontgaramond\fontsize{13}{18}\selectfont\color{Ink}
Across languages, certain words hold a feeling, a ritual, or a way of seeing
that takes a whole sentence to explain in English. This collection gathers
words like these—not as rules for living, but as invitations to notice: the
sweetness of an ordinary afternoon, the courage to continue, the space to
breathe, and the beauty of what changes.

\vspace{0.8em}
Each word carries its own history and nuance. The short definition gives its
meaning; the italic line is a poetic interpretation written for this book.
Pronunciations are approximate reading guides. Flags offer cultural or
geographic context, not exclusive claims of ownership. Turn the page, and let
the illustration carry the feeling.
\end{minipage}
